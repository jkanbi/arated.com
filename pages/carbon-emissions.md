# Carbon Emissions

## Eco-Friendly Offices

Our current office, situated in a residential area, showcases the best in sustainable energy solutions. It proudly harnesses Solar PV Energy with Battery Storage, ensuring a consistent and clean power supply. The water heating system combines the efficiency of Solar Thermal technology with the innovation of an Air Source Heat Pump. This same Air Source Heat Pump also provides comfortable and eco-friendly heating throughout the space. We're delighted to have achieved an EPC A Rated status, reflecting our commitment to energy efficiency.

## Sustainable Systems and Software

We actively seek out services that align with our values, prioritizing those powered by renewable and sustainable energy sources. This approach ensures that our digital footprint remains as green as our physical one.

## Eco-Conscious Products

Sustainability is at the forefront of our product selection process. We place a high value on longevity, choosing items that will serve us well for years to come. Our approach is both environmentally conscious and practical, considering the overall impact and efficiency of each product we use in our business.
